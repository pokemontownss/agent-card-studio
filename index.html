HOW IT WORKS

Three steps to an on-chain card

Designing runs in your browser;
minting goes through your wallet.

STEP 01◈

Connect your wallet

Tap the Connect Wallet button above and approve in your wallet. Make sure your network is BNB Smart Chain (56). This page never asks for a private key or seed phrase.

STEP 02✳

Personalize your card

Write your Agent ID name, pick a Guardian, Builder, or Explorer persona, then set the color. The preview updates instantly.

Check the live mint price from the contract, approve the transaction in your wallet, and your card is issued as an NFT on BNB Chain — straight into your wallet.

FREQUENTLY ASKED

Frequently asked questions

Short answers about minting NFTs on this page. Transactions here are real on BNB Chain and BNB payments are final.

Is this a real mint?

Yes. Once the owner deploys the contract, the Mint button sends a real transaction to the AgentCard contract on BNB Chain and your card is issued as an ERC-721 NFT. Before the contract is deployed, the mint button stays disabled and the page does not pretend minting works.

Is it safe? Do I need to enter a private key?

You never enter a private key or seed phrase on this page. You only approve transactions in your own wallet (MetaMask, Trust Wallet, or Binance Wallet), and you can check the price and gas in your wallet before approving.

Do I need real BNB?

Yes, and there are two editions. Free Edition: just a 0.0002 BNB anti-sybil fee (starting price, changeable by the owner), max 1 free mint per wallet, standard design. Paid Edition: 0.002 BNB (starting price), a premium gold holo design with power +30, unlimited mints — but every wallet can mint at most 2 cards in total across both editions. Both add BNB Chain network gas, are paid in BNB, and are final. The live numbers appear on the confirmation page once the contract is deployed.

Where is my card after minting?

In your wallet, as an NFT. Its name, persona, color, and edition are stored in the contract, and the NFT image is rendered on-chain by the contract itself. You can view it in the My Cards section of this page or on BscScan.

Is this an official Binance project?

No. Agent Card Studio is a community project. Its cards are collectible digital cards on BNB Chain — not an official Agent ID issued by Binance — and it is not affiliated with Binance.
