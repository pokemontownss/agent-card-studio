Deploy the contracts

This page deploys all four Agent Card Studio contracts from your own wallet, in order: the AgentCard NFT, the $ACARD token, and the Burn & Stake reward contracts — then funds the reward pools and links them. Mint opens by default on the NFT contract, so there is no separate open-mint step. Every transaction is signed in your wallet; this page never asks for a seed phrase or private key.

Not connected. Connect the owner wallet to begin.

Deploy AgentCard (NFT)

The card collection: 4,444 supply, free mint 0.0002 BNB (1× per wallet), paid mint 0.002 BNB, max 2 cards per wallet. Mint is open by default.

04

Deploy Stake contract

Pays 500 $ACARD per day for every staked card. Needs the $ACARD address from step 02.

Waiting — deploy $ACARD first.

BscScan ↗
05

Fund the reward pools

Transfers $ACARD from your wallet into the reward contracts. Defaults cover the full collection: 4,444 cards × 50,000 for burning, and 200,000,000 for staking.

Waiting — deploy Burn and Stake first.

06

Link the NFT contract

Points Burn and Stake at the AgentCard NFT from step 01 — two transactions, one per contract. After this, burning and staking work.

Waiting — deploy steps 01, 03 and 04 first.

Contract addresses

When all steps are done, copy these four addresses and send them to Muse — they get wired into agentcard.cloud permanently so the mint, burn and stake features go live for everyone. Addresses are also kept in this browser, so you can reload this page without losing them.
